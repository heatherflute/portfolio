About

A teaching background that informs how I design and build.

Before moving into web development, I worked in Montessori education in Paris and South Africa. That experience made me care deeply about communication, usability, and how people move through information.

Now I bring that same mindset into front-end work: thoughtful structure, responsive layout, and a visual style that feels clear rather than overwhelming.

  • Semantic HTML5 and clean document structure
  • Responsive layout with Bootstrap grid + custom CSS
  • Accessible typography and strong spacing
  • Simple interfaces with minimal JavaScript

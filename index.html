About me

From Montessori to tech.

My story is anytrhing but linear, — and I think that makes it more interesting.

For eight years I was a Montessori teacher in Paris — the city of love, and the city where I fell in love with the idea that the best way to understand something is to make it. Montessori isn't just a teaching style; it's a philosophy of hands-on learning.

The Montessori philosophy followed me out of the classroom. One late-night rabbit hole about how websites actually work turned into another, and before I knew it I was writing HTML at 1am and genuinely enjoying it. I taught myself to code, one project at a time, using Codecademy and willpower.

Now I'm channelling that same love of learning and building into a career in tech. I care deeply about accessible, intuitive interfaces — ones that feel as natural as picking up a Montessori material. The educator never left; she just switched tools.
